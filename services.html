Capability 02

Kitchen Cabinet Solutions

Custom cabinetry made to fit the room and how it is used. Designed for beauty, durability, and daily functional ease.

We build kitchen cabinetry and storage to suit the layout, finishes, and everyday use of each space. Our workshop craftsmen fit the cabinetry on site and check its precise alignment with surrounding walls and finishes.

Cabinetry & Storage

Ergonomic base cabinets, full-height pantry storage, appliance garages, and concealed drawers.

Materials & Hardware

High-pressure laminates, anti-scratch acrylics, sintered stone, quartz counters, and soft-close hinges.

On-Site Fitting & Calibration

Our own master carpenters handle on-site installation, ensuring zero gaps, seamless filler strips, and perfect door alignment.

Capability 03

Electrical & M&E Engineering

Complete electrical wiring, lighting placement, plumbing, and HVAC routing executed by our in-house technicians.

Mechanical and electrical integrity forms the backbone of any luxury renovation. We coordinate wiring and ducting concurrently with carpentry shop drawings, so light points and AC cassettes sit in exact harmony with your furniture.

Lighting Design & Placement: Ambient LED cove strips, recessed magnetic track lights, and statement pendant drops.
Power Points & Distribution: Dedicated appliance power circuits, USB-C wall outlets, and 3-phase DB upgrades.
Plumbing & Sanitary: Concealed hot/cold water piping, water pressure booster pump integration, and drainage traps.
HVAC & Ceiling Cassettes: Concealed copper piping, condensate drainage, and ceiling cassette air-conditioning integration.
