Four Practical Stages

End-to-End Delivery Architecture

Our delivery framework bridges the usual gap between site contractors and off-site fabricators.

01

Consultation

Discuss your needs, the space, and the intended scope during a dedicated on-site meeting.

02

Proposal

Agree on comprehensive scope, materials, transparent line-item costing, and timeline schedule.

03

Execution

Coordinate site works, off-site workshop fabrication, careful installation, and progress updates.

04

Handover

Inspect completed works, test functional hardware, and perform defect audits before handover.

01
Initial Briefing

Consultation & Site Assessment

Every great space starts with active listening and technical site evaluation. During our consultation phase:

  • On-site dimensional survey and photographic documentation of existing structural layout.
  • Review of architectural floor plans, ceiling heights, electrical DB capacity, and plumbing lines.
  • Clarification of storage requirements, ergonomics, countertop selections, and appliance specifications.
02
Costing & Engineering

Proposal & Technical Planning

We eliminate ambiguity. Our proposals provide an exact blueprint of what is being built, how it is made, and what it costs:

  • Transparent, line-item quotation covering general contracting, electrical, plumbing, and cabinetry.
  • Material selection guide: quartz vs. sintered stone, laminate samples, soft-close hardware specifications.
  • Locked milestone schedule defining demolition start, MEP rough-in, fabrication delivery, and final handover.
03
Build & Fabrication

Execution & Workshop Synergy

This is where our integrated model shines. While our site team clears, frames, and runs electrical conduits, our workshop fabricates the custom joinery concurrently:

  • Shared dimensions between site managers and carpentry workshop prevent mismatched cabinet carcasses.
  • Regular WhatsApp photo & video milestone updates keeping you informed on real progress.
  • Direct installation by the master carpenters who built your cabinetry, guaranteeing perfect fit and clean joinery.
04
Final Sign-Off

Handover & Defect-Free Delivery

We take pride in our finishing standards. Before handing over the keys, we perform exhaustive checks to ensure perfection:

  • Testing of every cabinet hinge, drawer slide, soft-close mechanism, and lighting switch.
  • Plumbing pressure testing, water leakage inspection, and AC drainage slope verification.
  • Joint walk-through audit with the client, comprehensive clean-up, and issuance of warranty documentation.
